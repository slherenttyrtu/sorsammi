Support

Questions, a bug, an idea: write to sorsammi@gmail.com. If something isn't working, include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Cancel or change your subscription: iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Premium is missing on a new phone: Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Pairing: Both phones need iCloud turned on, signed in to two different iCloud accounts. One of you taps "Pair with your partner" in Settings and sends the code. The other opens the same screen, taps "Enter a code" and types it within 24 hours.

No morning question: Make sure notifications for Sorsam Mı? are on in iPhone Settings > Notifications, and that "The day's question" is on in the app's Settings.

Your notes: Your notes stay on your phone. If you delete the app, they're deleted too. We have no copy to restore.
