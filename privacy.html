Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short: Sorsam Mı? asks for no account, and we have no server. Your notes stay on your phone. If you pair with your partner, your "We talked" records and Premium status are shared between the two of you through Apple's iCloud. We can't see them.

Who is responsible: NETY4WORK Yazılım Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. Contact: sorsammi@gmail.com.

Data kept on your phone

  • "We talked" records, the notes you write, your streak count, your appearance and notification settings.
  • This data is stored on your device and is not sent to us. The one exception: if you pair, your "We talked" records and Premium status are shared with your partner as described below. The widgets read today's question, your streak, your appearance setting and (if you add the "From the journal" widget) one past note from a shared area of this same app on your phone. This happens on the device; nothing leaves it. You choose whether to add the widget that shows a note.
  • If you delete the app, this data is deleted from your phone too. We hold no copy, so we cannot restore it.

Pairing with your partner (optional): Pairing runs on Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). We have no server and can't see these records.

  • Shared: the days and times you tapped "We talked," and a random device ID that tells your two phones apart. If one of you buys Premium, Apple's signed purchase record is shared too (product, dates, transaction ID, storefront country, price, and the like, with no name or card details). That's how Premium unlocks on your partner's phone.
  • Not shared by the app: your notes, the questions, your name, your email. Apple's sharing system may show each participant the other's iCloud name. The app doesn't use it.
  • The records live in the inviting partner's iCloud account and are shared only with the other partner.
  • The invite code sits in a public iCloud area and is valid for 24 hours. Only the pairing link and an expiry time are stored with it, with no name or account details. It's deleted once your partner uses it or you get a new code.
  • You can unpair in the app's Settings. If the inviting partner unpairs, sharing stops and the shared records are deleted right away. If the invited partner unpairs, sharing stops right away, and the records are deleted the next time the app opens on the inviting partner's phone.
  • Deleting the app doesn't delete pairing records in iCloud, so unpair first.

What we do not collect: name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Notifications: If you allow them, the app schedules morning and evening local notifications on your phone. They are created on the device and are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the iPhone Settings app or in the app's Settings screen.

Subscriptions and payment: Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple (App Store). We never see or store your card or payment details. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. The app only learns on the device whether your subscription is active.

Children: The app is for couples; the App Store age rating is 13+. We do not knowingly collect data from children (we collect it from no one).

Your rights: Because we do not hold your data, access, correction and deletion are done in the app on your device or by deleting the app. For questions, write to sorsammi@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, the same applies under GDPR: we process no personal data on our side. You can delete pairing records by unpairing.

Changes: If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. If we ever start collecting data (for example cloud backup), we will ask for your permission first.
