Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short: Sorsam Mı? asks for no account and sends your data to no server. Your notes and "We talked" records stay on your phone.

Who is responsible: NETY4WORK Yazılım Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. Contact: sorsammi@gmail.com.

Data kept on your phone

  • "We talked" records, the notes you write, your streak count, your appearance and notification settings.
  • This data is stored on your device only. It is not sent to us, to another company or to another device. The widgets read today's question, your streak, your appearance setting and (if you add the "From the journal" widget) one past note from a shared area of this same app on your phone. This happens on the device; nothing leaves it. You choose whether to add the widget that shows a note.
  • If you delete the app, this data is deleted too. We hold no copy, so we cannot restore it.

What we do not collect: name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Notifications: If you allow them, the app schedules morning and evening local notifications on your phone. They are created on the device and are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the iPhone Settings app or in the app's Settings screen.

Subscriptions and payment: Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple (App Store). We never see or store your card or payment details. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. The app only learns on the device whether your subscription is active.

Children: The app is for couples; the App Store age rating is 13+. We do not knowingly collect data from children (we collect it from no one).

Your rights: Because we do not hold your data, access, correction and deletion are done in the app on your device or by deleting the app. For questions, write to sorsammi@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, the same applies under GDPR: we process no personal data on our side.

Changes: If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. If we ever start collecting data (for example partner matching or cloud backup), we will ask for your permission first.
